# 01 — Harvard Referencing Quick Guide

> The brief mandates **Harvard citation style**. Harvard has institutional variants — the
> most common UK standard is **Cite Them Right**. ⚠️ **Check your university library's
> own Harvard guide** and follow that; where it differs from this file, it wins.

---

## In-text citations

| Situation | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Author in parentheses | (Author, Year) | …loss aversion (Kahneman and Tversky, 1979). |
| Author in the sentence | Author (Year) | Kahneman and Tversky (1979) show that… |
| Direct quotation | (Author, Year, p. X) | …"losses loom larger than gains" (Kahneman and Tversky, 1979, p. 279). |
| Two authors | (Author and Author, Year) | (Tversky and Kahneman, 1974) |
| Three or more | (First *et al.*, Year) | (Güth *et al.*, 1982) |
| Two works at once | (A, Year; B, Year) | (Simon, 1955; Kahneman and Tversky, 1979) |
| Same author, same year | Year a/b | (Kahneman and Tversky, 1979a) |
| Secondary source | (A, Year, cited in B, Year) | Prefer the **primary source**; read it if you can |

**In a 300-word piece, a parenthetical citation is usually the cheaper option** — it costs
2–3 words rather than restructuring the sentence.

## Reference list

Alphabetical by author surname. Hanging indent. Verdana 11pt, 1.5 spacing, like everything else.

### Journal article
> Author, A.A. (Year) 'Title of article', *Title of Journal*, Volume(Issue), pp. x–y.

> Kahneman, D. and Tversky, A. (1979) 'Prospect theory: an analysis of decision under risk', *Econometrica*, 47(2), pp. 263–291.

### Journal article with DOI / accessed online
> Simon, H.A. (1955) 'A behavioral model of rational choice', *Quarterly Journal of Economics*, 69(1), pp. 99–118. doi: 10.2307/1884852.

### Book
> Author, A.A. (Year) *Title of Book*. Edition (if not 1st). Place of publication: Publisher.

> Kahneman, D. (2011) *Thinking, Fast and Slow*. London: Allen Lane.

### Chapter in an edited book
> Author, A.A. (Year) 'Title of chapter', in Editor, E. (ed.) *Title of Book*. Place: Publisher, pp. x–y.

> Frederick, S. (2002) 'Automated choice heuristics', in Gilovich, T., Griffin, D. and Kahneman, D. (eds.) *Heuristics and Biases*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press, pp. 548–558.

### Lecture / module material
> Lecturer, A. (Year) '*Lecture title*' [Lecture]. Module Code: Module Name. Institution. Date.

⚠️ Citing lecture slides is sometimes discouraged — they are a secondary source. **Cite the
primary paper wherever possible.** If the module theory comes from a paper, cite the paper.

### Website
> Author/Organisation (Year) *Title of page*. Available at: URL (Accessed: DD Month YYYY).

## Common errors to avoid

| ❌ Wrong | ✅ Right |
|---|---|
| (Kahneman & Tversky, 1979) | (Kahneman and Tversky, 1979) — Harvard spells out "and" in text |
| Kahneman, D. & Tversky, A. (1979) | Kahneman, D. and Tversky, A. (1979) in the reference list |
| 'Prospect Theory: An Analysis Of Decision Under Risk' | Sentence case for article titles |
| *Prospect theory: an analysis…* (italicised) | Article titles in **'single quotes'**; **journal name** italicised |
| p. 263-291 | pp. 263–291 (double "pp.", en dash) |
| A citation in the text with no entry in the list | Every in-text citation must appear in the list, and vice versa |
| Citing Kahneman (2011) for prospect theory | Cite the 1979 *Econometrica* paper — the primary source |

## Checks before submission

- [ ] Every in-text citation has a reference list entry
- [ ] Every reference list entry is cited in the text
- [ ] Alphabetical order by surname
- [ ] Journal names italicised; article titles in single quotes, sentence case
- [ ] Page ranges use en dashes and `pp.`
- [ ] Volume(Issue) format correct
- [ ] "and" not "&" throughout
- [ ] Reference list in Verdana 11pt, 1.5 spaced
- [ ] **Checked against the university's own Harvard guide**
