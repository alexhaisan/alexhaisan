## Verification procedure

For each reference actually used in the submission:

1. Find the paper (library database, Google Scholar, JSTOR)
2. Confirm: authors, year, exact title, journal, **volume(issue)**, **page range**
3. Confirm it appears on the module reading list → mark ⭐
4. Update status to `✅ verified`
5. Only then paste it into the submission

**Only a handful of references will actually be cited in a 300-word piece.** Verify those
properly rather than the whole list.
